# Acknowledgements

## WB2CBA — TinyDX

Special thanks to **Barbaros Aşuroğlu, WB2CBA**, for his work on **TinyDX — Tiny Digital Modes HF Transceiver** and his wider QRP experimentation.

TinyDX was an important inspiration for AT-DX1, particularly the idea that a very small USB-oriented HF digital transceiver can still be genuinely useful in the field. AT-DX1 also specifically acknowledges WB2CBA's practical use of the **74ACT244 logic-buffer IC as a compact HF RF PA/driver**, which strongly influenced the transmitter direction taken here.

Original TinyDX repository:

https://github.com/WB2CBA/TinyDX---Tiny-Digital-Modes-HF-Transceiver

TinyDX design article:

https://antrak.org.tr/blog/tinydx-my-quest-for-designing-the-smallest-digital-modes-hf-transceiver/

Thank you, Barb, for sharing the project and helping make this kind of amateur-radio experimentation accessible.

AT-DX1 is not affiliated with or endorsed by WB2CBA.

## Open-source software and libraries

The current firmware uses the Arduino ecosystem and libraries including the Earle Philhower Arduino-Pico core, Adafruit TinyUSB and Arduino Audio Tools. Those projects remain under their respective licences; AT-DX1's MIT licence applies only to original AT-DX1 software to the extent that ZL1DMM has the right to license it.

## Amateur-radio community

AT-DX1 also benefits from the amateur-radio community's long tradition of QRP/QRPp experimentation, direct-conversion and quadrature-sampling receivers, digital modes, software-defined radio, CW experimentation, portable POTA/SOTA operation and openly shared technical work.
